Behind the Lens

We’re Huntur and Ian — two friends who have always shared a passion for photography, videography, and creating something of our own. After years of talking about starting a business together, we decided to turn that passion into reality.

For us, it’s about more than taking photos or videos. It’s about capturing real moments, telling stories, and creating something worth remembering.
